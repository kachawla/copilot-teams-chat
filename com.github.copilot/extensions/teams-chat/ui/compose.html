To post to one chat or channel: in Teams, open Apps > Workflows (or make.powerautomate.com > Templates), choose Send webhook alerts to a chat or to a channel, pick where to post, and copy the URL it shows. Then ask Copilot: "Save this Teams workflow as team chat: <URL>".

To message anyone by email, as yourself: build the workflow under Send to anyone in the extension's README, then ask Copilot: "Save this as my direct Teams workflow: <URL>".
